Cave, rock hill and Holy Family

Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 7 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Make a cardboard mock-up of the arch (3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high) and the cave plan (4 × 2.5 ft). STACK (cave, hill, house): the cave floor is at deck level and the arch is 2.5 ft high. The hill top and the house floor are at +3.3 ft, so about 10 in of rock sits above the arch. The room is 1.5 ft high, so its roof is at +4.8 ft. Make a cardboard mock-up of cave + house first and look at it from the public spot before cutting the hill or the wood.
  2. Cut the plywood base and glue 4 in thermocol layers to the hill outline (see the top view) up to 3.3 ft, sealing every layer with PVA. Keep 1 ft free behind for cables.
  3. Mark the cave with its centre at x 4.4 ft, y 7.4 ft, turned 33°. Cut the 4 × 2.5 ft inside with the hot knife and the arch to the template, leaving about 10 in of rock above the arch.
  4. Carve horizontal strata and cracks on the outside; keep the back of the hill against the step.
  5. Mesh the arch and overhangs. Coat with plaster of Paris + PVA, 3 mm thick; press on sand/grout, real pebbles and dry grass while wet.
  6. Dry-brush sand, then ochre, then olive; darker in the cracks. Paint the cave inside near-black; keep the rock around the cave one shade darker so the gold glows.
  7. Cut the light holes and leave a 1 ft cable tail at each. Make the manger and the clay lamp, lay straw, then place Mary, Joseph, baby, ox and donkey last (group about 3 × 1.2 ft).
  8. Split the unit into numbered pieces, none wider than 4 ft, and check each passes the church door.

Before you start

  • Wear a mask for plaster dust; cut thermocol outdoors; fire-retardant spray every cut surface.
  • Decide: buy new 2 ft figures or reuse and repaint the 2023 set.
  • Make a cardboard mock-up of cave + house first (see the stack note).

Materials to get

Thermocol 4 in, 8 × 4 ft (9 sheets), Plaster of Paris 25 kg (4 bags), Wall putty / tile grout + sand (3 bags), PVA glue (5 L), Emulsion / acrylic earth colours + brushes (8 L), Chicken mesh + binding wire (lot), Straw, dry grass tufts (lot), Plywood 12 mm base, 8 × 4 ft (2 sheets), Holy Family, 2 ft figures: Mary, Joseph, baby (set of 3), Ox and donkey, about 1 ft (2), Manger (thermocol + plaster) + clay lamp (1)

Tools: Hot-wire cutter, hot knife, trowel, mesh cutters, glue gun, brushes, sculpting clay, dust mask

  • From V the manger and baby are visible and not hidden by the arch.

1. Plywood base for the hill

Box: 7 ft (84 in) W × 8.2 ft (98 in) D × 0.05 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: About 7 × 6 ft in pieces no wider than 4 ft, x 2.0–9.0, y 1.7–9.9 (irregular, see the top view).

Materials: 12 mm plywood, 2 sheets.

Reuse: Reuse 2023 plywood. Number the pieces so the hill comes apart and goes back together next year.

How to make it:

  1. Draw the hill outline on the deck with chalk from the top view.
  2. Cut the base in 2–3 pieces along straight lines, none wider than 4 ft.
  3. Number each piece B1, B2, B3 underneath.
  4. Cut a 4 × 2.5 ft notch where the cave floor goes; the cave floor is the deck itself.

2. Rock hill

Box: 7 ft (84 in) W × 8.2 ft (98 in) D × 3.3 ft (40 in) H

Size and place: About 7 × 6 ft base, up to 3.3 ft high, back against the step; keep 1 ft free behind for cables.

Materials: Thermocol 4 in (9 sheets), PVA glue, chicken mesh, plaster of Paris, grout + sand, pebbles, earth paints.

Reuse: Make it in the numbered pieces of the base so it can be stored and reused next year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut thermocol layers to the outline, each layer about 2 in smaller than the one below; glue with PVA.
  2. Build up about 10 layers to 3.3 ft at the highest point (the house sits on top).
  3. Make the top flat over a 4 × 2 ft area for the house (block C).
  4. Carve horizontal strata and cracks with a hot knife; round the layer steps.
  5. Pin chicken mesh over overhangs; coat everything with PoP + PVA 3 mm thick and press on grout, sand and pebbles.
  6. Dry-brush sand, then ochre, then olive; darker in cracks.

3. Cave

Box: 4 ft (48 in) W × 2.5 ft (30 in) D × 2.5 ft (30 in) H

Size and place: Inside 4 ft long × 2.5 ft deep, arch 3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high at deck level; centre x 4.4, y 7.4; turned 33° toward V; about 10 in of rock above the arch.

Materials: Cardboard for the template, the hill thermocol, PoP, near-black paint.

Reuse: Keep the cardboard arch template for next year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut a cardboard arch template 3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high with a rounded top.
  2. Mark the cave centre on the deck (x 4.4, y 7.4) and draw the 4 × 2.5 ft floor turned 33° toward V.
  3. Cut the inside out of the thermocol layers with a hot knife; follow the template at the mouth.
  4. Check about 10 in of rock remains above the arch.
  5. Plaster inside, then paint near-black; rock around the mouth one shade darker than the hill.
  6. Drill holes for the 2 cave lights (above the arch, behind the manger), the manger spot and the cross-shadow lamp; leave 1 ft cable tails.

4. Manger

Box: 1 ft (12 in) W × 0.5 ft (6 in) D × 0.5 ft (6 in) H

Size and place: About 1 ft long × 6 in wide × 6 in high, in the cave between Mary and Joseph.

Materials: Wood offcuts (or thermocol) + PoP, straw.

Reuse: Wood manger lasts many years; reuse the 2023 one if you have it.

How to make it:

  1. Cut two 1 ft × 6 in sides and two 6 × 6 in ends; screw into a box that is wider at the top.
  2. Add 4 short legs 2 in long.
  3. Coat with a thin layer of PoP for a stone look; paint limestone and dry-brush sand.
  4. Fill with straw so it hangs over the edges.

5. Clay lamp

Box: 0.25 ft (3 in) W × 0.15 ft (2 in) D × 0.1 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: About 3 in long, on a ledge inside the cave.

Materials: Air-dry clay, ochre paint.

Reuse: Keep it for next year.

How to make it:

  1. Shape a small oval bowl with a pinched spout.
  2. Let it dry 2 days; paint ochre.
  3. Place it on a rock ledge; the warm cave light makes it glow.

6. Mary, Joseph, baby Jesus

Box: 3 ft (36 in) W × 1.2 ft (14 in) D × 2 ft (24 in) H

Size and place: Hero scale 1:3: standing figures about 2 ft tall; group about 3 ft × 1.2 ft at x 2.7–5.9, y 6.2–8.8, turned 33° toward V.

Materials: Reuse the 2023 figures and repaint; Mary blue #3A5BA8 and cream, Joseph ochre, baby warm white.

Reuse: Reuse first; repaint only the cloth, not the faces.

How to make it:

  1. Check the 2023 figures: clean, fix chips with putty, repaint cloth in the palette colours.
  2. Mary kneels left of the manger, Joseph stands right holding a small lantern, baby in the manger.
  3. Set them last, after the cave is painted and dry.
  4. Look from V: the baby must be visible and not hidden by the arch.
