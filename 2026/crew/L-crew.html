Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Mark V on the church floor: 3 ft to the left of and 3 ft in front of the front-left corner of the stage.
  2. Make the two olive trees (wire, jute, paper, dried leaves) with umber trunks; darker and less saturated than the field behind them.
  3. Place olive 23 at x 1.0, y 14.2 and olive 24 at x 5.4, y 14.9 as the dark framing in the front corners.
  4. Make the plaque 2 × 0.4 ft with 12 hidden details and place it at the front edge.
  5. Curve the rope barrier 5 ft from the stage front, with 4 posts; keep a 4 ft clear aisle for wheelchairs.
  6. Place the signs ("Please stand here", "The crib shows every 10 minutes", "Find 12 details").
  7. Confirm the extinguisher location and the exit with the church; run cables under a ramp or tape them flat.
  8. Walk through with six people at V to confirm the sightlines.

Before you start

  • Confirm the extinguisher location and the exit with the church.
  • Print the signs (A4, large type).

Materials to get

Tools: Rope, posts, floor tape, printer, wire, jute
