Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 8 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Measure the real ceiling height: the drape drop is the ceiling height minus 7 ft.
  2. Send both banner PDFs (2026/banners/) to the printer at full size: 9 × 7 ft and 16 × 7 ft, matt finish, eyelets every 1 ft along the top edge.
  3. Fit the ceiling track and rope backup along both walls; fix a rope or batten at 7 ft above the deck on both walls for the banners.
  4. Hang the back banner first, bottom edge on the deck, tied through its eyelets; then the right banner so its left edge meets the back banner in the corner and the hills line up.
  5. Sew a pocket along the top of each banner for the star lights (block H fits them).
  6. Pleat the drape at 1.5× fullness and hang it on rings from the track so its hem laps the banner top by 6 in.
  7. Glue the gold stars in irregular clusters and fix the 12 white-and-silver angels to the drape.
  8. Check from the public spot: the banners hang flat, the corner join lines up, the pleats are even.

Before you start

  • Send the two print files in 2026/banners/ (PDF, full size) to the printer early; ask for matt finish, eyelets every 1 ft along the top.
  • Measure the real ceiling height; the drape drop is ceiling minus 7 ft.
  • Leave a pocket along the top of each banner for the star lights (block H).

Materials to get

Printed banners: matt flex or fabric, 9 × 7 ft + 16 × 7 ft (print files ready) (175 sq ft), Blue drape, 6 ft drop × 25 ft (poly vs velvet) (~20 m), Gold stars: card + glitter + glue (120), Curtain track, rope, hooks, clamps (25 ft), Eyelets, cable ties, stitching + pleat tape (lot), Choir angels 2 ft, card or foam, white and silver paint (12)

Tools: Drill, rope, clamps, cable ties, sewing machine, scissors

Done when (tick each)

  • Both banners hang flat, bottom edge on the deck, top at 7 ft; the scene meets at the corner.
  • Drape pleats even at 1.5× fullness; the hem laps the banner top by 6 in.
  • Twelve painted angels in white and silver, no glow paint.
  • Gold stars in irregular clusters; no colour other than the palette.

Hands over to

Hangs on both walls. H fits the star points and the night-sky wash. Houses (G) and the ridge (K) stand in front of the right banner.

1. Ceiling track, rope and banner line

Box: 25 ft (300 in) W × 0.2 ft (2 in) D × 0.2 ft (2 in) H

Size and place: Track 25 ft along the back wall (9 ft) and right wall (16 ft), about 13 ft above the deck; a rope or batten at 7 ft for the banners.

Materials: Curtain track 25 ft, rope backup, hooks, clamps, cable ties.

Reuse: Reuse the parish track if any.

How to make it:

  1. Measure the real ceiling height.
  2. Fix the track along both walls; tie a rope backup under it.
  3. Stretch a rope (or fix a batten) along both walls exactly 7 ft above the deck.

2. Back-wall banner

Box: 9 ft (108 in) W × 0.02 ft (0 in) D × 7 ft (84 in) H

Size and place: 9 ft wide × 7 ft high (108 × 84 in); bottom edge on the deck; the bottom 18 in sits behind the raised level.

Materials: Printed matt flex or fabric from 2026/banners/back-banner-9x7ft.pdf, eyelets every 1 ft, cable ties.

Reuse: Roll (do not fold) and store; reuse next year.

How to make it:

  1. Send the PDF to the printer at full size, matt, with eyelets along the top.
  2. Tie the top eyelets to the 7 ft rope, starting from the corner.
  3. Pull the bottom edge down to the deck; tape it flat behind the raised level.

3. Right-wall banner

Box: 16 ft (192 in) W × 0.02 ft (0 in) D × 7 ft (84 in) H

Size and place: 16 ft wide × 7 ft high (192 × 84 in); bottom edge on the deck; left edge meets the back banner in the corner.

Materials: Printed matt flex or fabric from 2026/banners/right-banner-16x7ft.pdf, eyelets every 1 ft, cable ties.

Reuse: Roll and store; reuse next year.

How to make it:

  1. Tie the top eyelets to the 7 ft rope, starting at the corner.
  2. Line up the painted hills with the back banner at the corner.
  3. Smooth it flat; the houses (G) and ridge (K) stand in front later.

4. Blue drape

Box: 25 ft (300 in) W × 0.3 ft (4 in) D × 6 ft (72 in) H

Size and place: From 7 ft up to the ceiling: about 6 ft drop; 25 ft run; 1.5× fullness (about 20 m); hem laps the banner top by 6 in.

Materials: Midnight-blue polyester or velvet, pleat tape, rings.

Reuse: Reusable every year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut to ceiling height minus 7 ft, plus 6 in overlap and 4 in hem.
  2. Sew pleat tape along the top; hem the bottom.
  3. Hang on rings on the track; check the hem laps the banner by 6 in.

5. Light pocket

Box: 25 ft (300 in) W × 0.05 ft (1 in) D × 0.33 ft (4 in) H

Size and place: Along the top of each banner, both walls.

Materials: Fabric strip 4 in wide, 25 ft.

How to make it:

  1. Sew or pin a 4 in strip behind the top of each banner as a pocket for the star lights.

6. Gold stars ×120

Box: 0.25 ft (3 in) W × 0.01 ft (0 in) D × 0.25 ft (3 in) H

Size and place: Sizes 1–3 in; irregular clusters on the drape.

Materials: Card, gold glitter, glue, double-sided tape.

Reuse: Peel off and keep.

How to make it:

  1. Cut stars from card (40 each of 1, 2, 3 in).
  2. Glue glitter; dry.
  3. Tape in clusters on the drape, denser near the star corner.

7. Choir angels ×12

Box: 1 ft (12 in) W × 0.05 ft (1 in) D × 2 ft (24 in) H

Size and place: 2 ft tall flat cut-outs on the drape.

Materials: Foam board or card, white and silver paint.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Draw one angel 2 ft tall; cut; trace 11 more.
  2. Paint white with silver edges.
  3. Pin to the drape in a soft arc.
