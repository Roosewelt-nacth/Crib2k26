Two-layer stage

Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 6 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. On site: measure the real deck (2 ft high assumed, free 9 × 16 ft, level) and ask the stage supplier whether you may screw into it. If not, clamp the boxes to the deck edge and weigh them down. Mark the 9 × 16 ft rectangle with tape and the step line 11.5 ft from the front.
  2. Workshop: build two frames 4.5 ft × 4.5 ft from 2 × 2 in battens, fixed with screws (not glue), with cross battens at 1.5 ft centres both ways. Number each frame in marker.
  3. Skin each frame: top 12 mm plywood, front and end faces 9 mm. Make the step face 9 ft long × 1.5 ft high; it will be faced with dry-stone by block E.
  4. Cut the three hatch lids (sizes in the cut list) and leave them loose, labelled A, B, C.
  5. Place the two frames against the back wall on the deck, side by side, and tie them together. The front faces line up in one straight step across the full 9 ft.
  6. Fix the black skirting with Velcro along the front edge (9 ft) and the left edge (16 ft), 2 ft drop, down to the floor.
  7. Spray all cloth and any thermocol with fire retardant (4 L in total).
  8. Check: two people on any part without flex, the step is 1.5 ft high all along, and every piece passes through the church door.

Before you start

  • Measure the real deck: height (2 ft assumed), size, level, and whether you may screw into it. If not, clamp and weigh the boxes down.
  • Mark the 9 × 16 ft grid and the step line 11.5 ft from the front with tape.

1. Upper box U1 (left) and U2 (right): make 2 the same

Box: 4.5 ft (54 in) W × 4.5 ft (54 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: 4.5 ft × 4.5 ft × 1.5 ft high each. U1 at x 0–4.5, U2 at x 4.5–9, both y 0–4.5 against the back wall.

Materials: 2 × 2 in pine battens (about 45 ft per box), 12 mm plywood top 4.5 × 4.5 ft, 2½ in screws, wood glue only on the top.

Reuse: Reuse 2023 plywood and battens first. Screw, do not nail, so the boxes come apart and are used again next year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut 4 battens 4.5 ft and 4 battens 4.2 ft; screw them into two square rings 4.5 × 4.5 ft (top ring and bottom ring).
  2. Cut 9 legs 1.5 ft minus 12 mm (17½ in). Screw one in each corner, one mid-side and one in the centre, between the two rings.
  3. Add cross battens across the top ring every 1.5 ft both ways, so no unsupported span is wider than 1.5 ft.
  4. Screw the 12 mm top on with a screw every 6 in around the edge and along every batten.
  5. Write U1 or U2 and an arrow "FRONT" on the inside in marker.
  6. Test: stand on every corner and the middle. No flex, no rocking.

2. Step face and end faces

Box: 9 ft (108 in) W × 0.06 ft (1 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: Step face 9 ft long × 1.5 ft high along y 4.5 (11.5 ft from the front). Left end face 4.5 × 1.5 ft at x 0.

Materials: 9 mm plywood: one piece 8 ft × 1.5 ft + one piece 1 ft × 1.5 ft for the step; one piece 4.5 × 1.5 ft for the left end.

Reuse: Offcuts from the 2023 sheets are fine; joins are hidden by the stone facing (block E).

How to make it:

  1. Cut the pieces with a straight saw line; mark the join 8 ft from the left.
  2. Screw the step face to the front battens of U1 and U2 with a screw every 8 in.
  3. Screw the left end face to U1 (the right end is against the church wall; no face needed).
  4. Check the face is straight along the full 9 ft with a string line.

3. Hatch lids A, B, C

Box: 1.2 ft (14 in) W × 0.8 ft (10 in) D × 0.05 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: A 1.2 × 0.8 ft at x 0–1.2, y 0.2–1.0 (light box) · B 1 × 0.8 ft at x 1.0–2.0, y 3.6–4.4 (field junction) · C 1 × 1 ft at x 0–1, y 15–16 on the deck (speaker).

Materials: 12 mm plywood offcuts, 1 × 1 in pine strip for a lip, finger hole.

Reuse: Lids are loose, so they come out and are reused as they are.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the openings in the U1 top for A and B before fitting the top (or with a jigsaw after).
  2. Cut each lid 2 mm smaller than its opening all round.
  3. Screw 1 × 1 in strips under the opening edges so the lid rests flush with the top.
  4. Drill a 1 in finger hole in each lid and write A, B or C on it.
  5. Lid C sits on the deck at the front-left corner over the speaker; just mark its place with tape.

4. Black skirting

Box: 25 ft (300 in) W × 0.1 ft (1 in) D × 2 ft (24 in) H

Size and place: 25 ft run: 9 ft along the front edge + 16 ft along the left edge; 2 ft drop to the church floor.

Materials: Black pleated cloth 25 ft × 2 ft, sew-on Velcro 25 ft + stick-on Velcro 25 ft, fire-retardant spray.

Reuse: Reuse the 2023 skirting if it is long enough; Velcro lets it come off clean from the rented deck.

How to make it:

  1. Stick the hook Velcro along the deck edge, 1 in below the top, after wiping it clean.
  2. Sew the loop Velcro to the top hem of the skirting.
  3. Spray the cloth with fire retardant and let it dry.
  4. Press it on from the back-left corner, round the front-left corner, to the front-right corner.
  5. Check the bottom just touches the floor and nothing shows under it.

5. Floor grid and step line

Box: 9 ft (108 in) W × 16 ft (192 in) D

Size and place: The 9 × 16 ft rectangle and a line at 11.5 ft from the front (the step).

Materials: Masking tape, measuring tape 5 m, marker.

Reuse: Nothing to buy.

How to make it:

  1. Measure the real deck: height (2 ft assumed), size, level, and ask whether you may screw into it.
  2. Tape lines every 1 ft along the left and front edges and write the numbers (x 0–9, y 0–16).
  3. Tape the step line straight across at y 4.5 (11.5 ft from the front).
  4. If you may not screw into the deck: clamp U1 and U2 to the deck edge and put 2 sandbags inside each box.

Selfie mirror (church floor)

Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 5 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Confirm there is clear church floor 6 ft to the left of the stage edge and 3 ft left of V, and that the aisle stays 4 ft clear.
  2. Mirror: build the frame 2 ft × 5 ft from 2 × 2 in battens with a 12 mm ply back; fix the acrylic mirror sheet and round and cover every edge.
  3. Fit a weighted base about 2.5 ft × 1.5 ft with rubber feet so the mirror cannot tip.
  4. Stand the mirror on the floor at x −6, y 19 and turn it about 23° toward the crib. Test with a phone: a person at V with their back to the crib should see the crib behind them. Adjust by up to 10° on the day.
  5. Place the sign and a floor marker for the photo spot; walk through with six people: the queue must not block the view of the crib from V.

Before you start

  • Check with the church that a floor mirror is allowed.
  • Costs are estimates; get quotes before buying the mirror sheet.

Materials to get

Acrylic mirror sheet 2 × 5 ft (shatter-safe, not glass) (1), Mirror frame (2 × 2 in + 12 mm ply back) and weighted base (1 set), Sign "Take a photo, share it" + floor marker (lot)

Tools: Saw, drill, staple gun, spirit level, phone for the angle test

Done when (tick each)

  • Mirror is anchored on its weighted base and cannot tip; all edges covered.
  • A person at V sees the crib behind them in the mirror.
  • The mirror and its queue do not block the view from V; 4 ft aisle clear.

Hands over to

The mirror stands on the church floor beside V and the rope barrier (L).

1. Selfie mirror

Box: 2 ft (24 in) W × 0.15 ft (2 in) D × 5 ft (60 in) H

Size and place: 2 ft × 5 ft acrylic mirror; frame 1.5 in thick; on the church floor at x −6, y 19, turned 23° toward the crib.

Materials: Acrylic mirror 2 × 5 ft, 2 × 2 in battens, 12 mm ply back, weighted base 2.5 × 1.5 ft, rubber feet.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Build the frame and screw the ply back.
  2. Stick the acrylic mirror on; cover every edge.
  3. Screw on the weighted base; check it cannot tip.
  4. Place and turn 23°; test with a phone from V.

2. Sign and marker

Box: 0.7 ft (8 in) W × 0.05 ft (1 in) D × 1 ft (12 in) H

Size and place: "Take a photo, share it" sign A4; floor marker at the photo spot.

Materials: Printed A4, stand, floor tape.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Place the sign beside the mirror; tape the marker; keep the aisle 4 ft clear.
