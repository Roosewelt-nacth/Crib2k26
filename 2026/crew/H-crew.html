Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 5 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Confirm the church socket behind the stage (exact spot and circuit rating) and collect the light-point list from every block.
  2. Bench build in the workshop: mount the MCB, RCCB, three switches, fuse block and the three power supplies in the box; label the rails R1, R2, R3 and the switches Lamps, Night sky, Glory.
  3. Bench test every light for 10 seconds on its own switch and check its colour against the list (warm gold, warm white, cool white, indigo).
  4. Cut and label the four trunks T1–T4 and the branch T1b to length; label both ends of every cable.
  5. Install the box at hatch A (back-left of the upper level) and run T1 left, T1b to the field junction, T2 along the back wall and up behind the drape to the ceiling feed, T3 down the right wall, T4 under the hill to the cave.
  6. Fit the warm lights (cave, manger spot, cross-shadow lamp, house, windows, campfire, path, lanterns) into the holes the other blocks left, with the dimmer knob on the box for the cave.
  7. Place the floods on their small stands (three wash floods on the upper level aimed up at the backdrop, two field floods) and clip the angel lamp to the sheepfold post; fit the night-blue gel.
  8. Hang the star burst light and the spiral strings from the ceiling feed with block I; tuck the star points behind the drape pockets from block J; fit the pond lights; set the speaker in hatch C.
  9. An electrician checks the box, earthing and RCCB before the first full run. There is no mains voltage inside the crib.
  10. Rehearse the run with one volunteer: Lamps low (dusk), Night sky on (night), cave dimmer up and Glory on for 20–30 s, all warm, then Night sky off and loop.

Before you start

  • Confirm the church socket behind the stage: its exact spot and circuit rating.
  • Collect the light-point list from every block (hole + 1 ft cable tail at each).
  • Colour rule: gold only in the cave, star, path, lamps and Magi lanterns. No red, bright green, pink, multicolour, flashing or UV lights.

Every object, one by one: size, materials, how to make it

1. Distribution box

Size and place: At hatch A, x 0–1.2, y 0.2–1.0, under the loose lid; one plug into the church socket behind the stage.

Materials: Metal box, 6 A MCB, 30 mA RCCB, 3 switches (Lamps, Night sky, Glory), fuse block, two 12 V 10 A and one 5 V 5 A supplies, cave dimmer, 1 m inlet lead.

Reuse: Keep the whole box built; it is reused as one unit next year.

How to make it:

  1. Mount MCB and RCCB at the inlet; then the three supplies.
  2. Wire R1 (warm lamps) to switch Lamps, R2 (floods, wash) to Night sky / Glory, R3 (5 V) to its own fuse.
  3. Label every switch and rail.
  4. An electrician checks earthing and the RCCB before use.

2. Trunks T1–T4

Size and place: T1 left 16 ft to hatch C (+ T1b 1.3 ft to hatch B) · T2 back 9 ft then 13 ft up to the ceiling · T3 right wall 15 ft · T4 to the cave 8 ft.

Materials: 2-core 1.5 mm² cable (trunks), 0.75 mm² (branches), connectors, cable ties, labels.

Reuse: Coil and label for next year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut each trunk 1 ft longer than listed.
  2. Label both ends (T1, T2 ...).
  3. Run them under the hill and along the walls; tape flat.

3. Warm lamps

Size and place: Cave COB ×2, manger spot, cross-shadow lamp, house lamps ×3, window lamps ×15, campfire, path strip 3 m, star 10 W, Magi lanterns.

Materials: 12 V warm LEDs 2200–2700 K (manger spot 2700–3000 K), flicker modules for house, windows, campfire.

Reuse: Reuse 2023 LEDs if warm white.

How to make it:

  1. Bench test each light 10 s on its switch; check the colour is warm.
  2. Push each into the hole the block left; connect to its trunk.
  3. Set the cave dimmer so the manger is the brightest spot.

4. Floods, wash and cool lights

Size and place: Wash floods ×3 on stands at (2.0, 0.8), (5.0, 0.8), (8.3, 2.0); field floods ×2 at (1.6, 6.4), (2.9, 8.6); angel clip lamp; star points; pond lights ×2.

Materials: 10 W 12 V floods, night-blue gel, small stands 10 in, clip lamp 5000 K.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Fit the gel on the three wash floods; aim them up at the drape.
  2. Hide the field floods behind rocks; aim at the shepherds.
  3. Clip the angel lamp on the sheepfold post.

5. Speaker

Size and place: 5 × 4 × 6 in at hatch C, x 0–0.3, y 15.2–15.6.

Materials: Bluetooth 10 W speaker, 5 V lead.

Reuse: Reuse any parish speaker.

How to make it:

  1. Place under lid C; play the carol loop at 60 dB or less at V.
