Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Wire everything on a bench board first. Test each channel for 10 seconds.
  2. Keep 12 V and 5 V rails separate, one fuse each, one common ground. Label every cable at both ends and leave a 1 ft service loop at each hatch.
  3. Path of light: cut a 14 mm groove along the path; fit the channel flush; solder the strip (5 V, GND, data) and seal with heat-shrink; add a 470 Ω resistor on the data line and a 1000 µF capacitor across the supply.
  4. Fix the frosted diffuser over the LEDs; glue fine sand on top and pebbles at the edges; the path must never cross a solid object.
  5. Run the data to hatch A. Test the chase at 20 pixels per second (a bright 8-pixel comet runs front edge to cave mouth in 7 s at 2:30).
  6. Program the 4-minute cue sheet (0:00 dusk, 0:30 night, 1:00 stars and mist, 1:40 angel, 2:30 path chase, 3:00 cave glow and cross-shadow, 3:30 all warm, 3:55 fade and loop). Add a start button and manual override.
  7. Set the audio to 60 dB or less at the public spot.
  8. An electrician checks earthing and the RCCB before the first full run. There is no mains voltage inside the crib.

Before you start

  • Wire everything on a bench board first; test every channel for 10 seconds.
  • Confirm the church socket and its circuit rating.
