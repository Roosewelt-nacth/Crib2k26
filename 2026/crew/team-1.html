Do exactly this

  1. Agree the angel choir place with block F: it stands on the upper level above the sheepfold.
  2. Face the step: cut three 3 ft × 1.5 ft strips of 2 in thermocol, carve them as dry-stone, glue real pebbles in, and fix them to the step face.
  3. Upper level: build the sheepfold (pebble ring 2.0 × 1.3 ft with a thorn gate) at x 0.4–2.4, y 1.75–3.05 and the watchtower at x 3.0, y 1.0, 1 ft across and 1.5 ft tall.
  4. Lower level: build the terrace wall 1.55 ft long, 3 in deep, 6 in high at y 6.3 from the left edge, the same way as the step wall.
  5. Cover both terraces with sand and soil, then patches of grass and moss. Paint olive, sand and limestone colours.
  6. Make the campfire from twigs and pebbles at x 1.0, y 8.3 and leave a light hole.
  7. Add the life of the field: the black tent at the back-left (x 0.05, y 4.6), 3 boulders, 3 thorn bushes, the stone trough by the pond side (y 9.75) and the 1.6 ft fig tree at x 2.25, y 9.55.
  8. Make 14 sheep (pipe-cleaner body + wool). Place the 3 shepherds and the sheep last, spread over both terraces, all facing the angels.
  9. Leave holes and 1 ft cable tails for the campfire light and the two field floods; check the pieces lift out one at a time.

1. Dry-stone step wall

Box: 9 ft (108 in) W × 0.17 ft (2 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: 9 ft long × 1.5 ft high, on the step face (y 4.5); the hill hides the middle part.

Materials: Thermocol 2 in (1½ sheets), PVA, pebbles, grout, paint.

Reuse: Make in 3 pieces of 3 ft so it is stored and reused.

How to make it:

  1. Cut three strips 3 ft × 1.5 ft from 2 in thermocol.
  2. Score stones about 4 × 2 in with a hot knife; vary the sizes.
  3. Glue real pebbles into some stones.
  4. Grout the joints, paint limestone, darken the joints.
  5. Glue or Velcro the pieces to the plywood step face.

2. Sheepfold

Box: 2 ft (24 in) W × 1.3 ft (16 in) D × 0.25 ft (3 in) H

Size and place: Ring 2.0 × 1.3 ft, walls 3 in high, with a 4 in thorn gate; upper level at x 0.4–2.4, y 1.75–3.05.

Materials: Thermocol 2 in base, pebbles, glue gun, dry thorny twigs.

Reuse: Build on its own base so it lifts off and is reused.

How to make it:

  1. Cut an oval base 2.0 × 1.3 ft from 2 in thermocol (or ply offcut).
  2. Glue pebbles in a ring 3 in high around the edge, leaving a 4 in gap.
  3. Tie dry twigs across the gap as the gate.
  4. Sand and moss inside; place it on the upper level.

3. Watchtower

Box: 1 ft (12 in) W × 1 ft (12 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: 1 ft across, 1.5 ft tall; upper level at x 3.0, y 1.0.

Materials: PVC pipe 4 in × 1.5 ft (or a cardboard tube), air-dry clay, pebbles.

Reuse: Reuse any old pipe.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the pipe 1.5 ft; glue a round base 1 ft across.
  2. Coat with clay and press pebbles in for a stone look.
  3. Cut a small door 4 in high at the bottom.
  4. Paint limestone and sand.

4. Ground cover

Box: 2.3 ft (28 in) W × 9 ft (108 in) D × 0.05 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: All open ground of the field, upper and lower levels on the left side.

Materials: Sand, soil, artificial grass pieces, moss, PVA.

Reuse: Sweep up and store the sand for next year.

How to make it:

  1. Brush PVA on the area in patches.
  2. Sprinkle soil and sand; press down.
  3. Add patches of grass and moss; paint olive and sand colours where bare.

5. Campfire

Box: 0.33 ft (4 in) W × 0.33 ft (4 in) D × 0.33 ft (4 in) H

Size and place: About 4 in across, 4 in tall, on the deck at x 1.0, y 8.3.

Materials: Small twigs, pebbles, glue gun; orange flicker LED from block H.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Drill a 10 mm hole in the deck spot for the light (ask block H).
  2. Ring 8 pebbles around the hole.
  3. Glue twigs in a cone over it, leaving gaps for the light.

6. Terrace wall

Box: 1.55 ft (19 in) W × 0.25 ft (3 in) D × 0.5 ft (6 in) H

Size and place: 1.55 ft long × 0.25 ft deep × 0.5 ft high; x 0–1.55, y 6.3–6.55 on the lower level.

Materials: Thermocol 2 in strip, pebbles, PVA, grout, paint.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Cut a strip 1.55 ft × 0.5 ft from 2 in thermocol.
  2. Score stones and glue pebbles in, the same way as the step wall.
  3. Grout, paint limestone, set it on the field at y 6.3.

7. Shepherds' tent (goat hair)

Box: 0.9 ft (11 in) W × 0.75 ft (9 in) D × 0.7 ft (8 in) H

Size and place: 0.9 ft wide × 0.75 ft deep × 0.7 ft high; x 0.05–0.95, y 4.6–5.35; open side toward the field.

Materials: Black or charcoal felt / old blanket 1.5 × 1.2 ft, 4 bamboo skewers, thread, a small rug scrap.

Reuse: Fold flat and reuse.

How to make it:

  1. Push 4 skewers in a rectangle 0.9 × 0.75 ft: back two 0.5 ft, front two 0.7 ft tall.
  2. Drape the cloth over them; let it sag between the poles.
  3. Tie guy threads to two small pebbles at the sides.
  4. Leave the front open; lay a rug scrap and a clay pot inside.

8. Boulders, thorn bushes and trough

Box: 0.3 ft (4 in) W × 0.3 ft (4 in) D × 0.3 ft (4 in) H

Size and place: Boulders 0.3 ft across × 0.3 ft high at (2.05, 4.95), (0.15, 7.75), (1.95, 8.0); thorn bushes 0.25 × 0.35 ft high at (1.55, 4.6), (0.1, 8.9), (1.85, 9.75); trough 0.7 × 0.22 × 0.2 ft at x 0.3, y 9.75.

Materials: Real stones (collected), dry thorny twigs, a wood offcut + clay for the trough.

Reuse: Free and reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Pick 3 rounded stones about 4 in across; glue them in place.
  2. Tie bunches of thorny twigs into low bushes; glue into the soil.
  3. Carve a groove in a 8 × 3 in offcut, coat with clay as stone, add blue plastic for water.

9. Fig tree

Box: 0.6 ft (7 in) W × 0.6 ft (7 in) D × 1.6 ft (19 in) H

Size and place: 1.6 ft tall, canopy 0.6 ft across; x 2.25, y 9.55, between the field and the path.

Materials: Twisted wire, jute, paper, broad green-grey leaves cut from card or dried leaves.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Twist 12 wires into a short thick trunk; splay into 5 branches.
  2. Wrap in jute; paint grey-brown.
  3. Glue broad leaves; keep it darker than the olive trees in front.

11. Sheep ×14

Box: 0.42 ft (5 in) W × 0.25 ft (3 in) D × 0.42 ft (5 in) H
