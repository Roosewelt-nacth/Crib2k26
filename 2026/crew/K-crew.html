Do exactly this

  1. Re-run the sightline check from the public spot V before fixing anything: the first layout hid both behind the house.
  2. Build the rock ridge 3.8 × 0.8 ft and 3 ft high from thermocol and plaster at x 7.8–8.6, y 5.2–9.0 along the right wall.
  3. Make 20 houses 3–5 in tall; paint them pale with blue shadows (tier D: paler, cooler, less saturated) and fix them on the ridge top at +3.0 ft.
  4. Fix the 3 × 0.3 ft plywood shelf at +3.6 ft on the right wall and paint a road line on it.
  5. Mount the caravan on the shelf and add tiny gold bead lanterns.
  6. Leave the micro-LED points and a 1 ft cable tail for block H.
  7. Check from the public spot that both are visible and not hidden by the house.
