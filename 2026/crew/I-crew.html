Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Measure the ceiling height and check its type (concrete, beam or plasterboard); plasterboard needs toggle anchors or a rope over a beam. Mark the 7 hang points with a plumb line.
  2. Build the 2 ft star on a 25 mm foam core with tinsel and a 10 W warm LED in the centre.
  3. Make the 1.8 ft ring and fix 60 strands of beads, each 6 ft long, one every 6°.
  4. Wind the 3 mm wire helix 2.5 ft wide, 3 turns, brace with cross-wires. Skin it with teased polyfill, sprayed with fire retardant.
  5. Fix the 30 angels (each on a 4 in loop) and tuck the 5 micro-LED strings into the clouds.
  6. Test everything lit at floor level first.
  7. Hang last, with two people and a ladder or scaffold: two lines per hang point, never a single line. Trim only when everything is lit.
  8. Keep the cascade at least 2 m from any sambrani holder.

Before you start

  • Measure the ceiling height and check its type (concrete, beam or plasterboard); this decides the anchors and the line lengths.
  • Ask the church about working at height and scaffold use.
