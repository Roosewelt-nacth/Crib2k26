Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Build the plywood box 2.4 × 1.4 ft and paint the inside matt black. Fix the screen face-up inside.
  2. Mount the acrylic at 45°, leaning toward the viewer at the top; the screen is lit below it and the glass reflects it.
  3. Cover everything except the acrylic with black cloth. The ridge (0.8 ft high) must hide the screen.
  4. Build the rock ridge from thermocol + plaster 2.4 × 1.0 ft × 0.8 ft; paint it as the rock around it.
  5. Make the 20 s angel video (white on black, mirrored left-right). Test at night from the public spot and tune the glass angle and brightness.
  6. Fit the mister tray behind the ridge lip with 3 cm of water and the float switch, on its own relay (on from 1:00 to 3:30). Keep a lip so mist never reaches the screen.
  7. Before each show wipe the acrylic with a microfibre cloth (fingerprints show as ghosts).

Before you start

  • Decide screen: reuse a tablet you own (free) or buy a monitor plus Pi Zero.
  • Make the 20 second looping angel video: white on pure black, mirrored left to right.

Materials to get

Clear acrylic 3 mm, 2.4 × 2 ft sheet (1), Screen: reuse an old tablet, or 10–13 in monitor + Pi Zero 2 W (1), Black cloth, plywood housing, black paint (lot), Ultrasonic mister discs ×2 + tray + 5 V fan + float (1 set), Ridge: thermocol + PoP + paint (1 sheet)

Tools: Jigsaw, black cloth, hot glue, tablet or monitor, spirit level
