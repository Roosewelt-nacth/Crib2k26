Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Check the cave arch (2.5 ft) and the +3.3 ft hill top with the mock-up (see block B). STACK (cave, hill, house): the cave floor is at deck level and the arch is 2.5 ft high. The hill top and the house floor are at +3.3 ft, so about 10 in of rock sits above the arch. The room is 1.5 ft high, so its roof is at +4.8 ft. Make a cardboard mock-up of cave + house first and look at it from the public spot before cutting thermocol.
  2. Cut the 12 mm plywood floor tray 4 × 2 ft. It rests on the hill top at +3.3 ft and on two 2 × 2 in posts that stand on the cave base; cut the posts so the floor is exactly 3.3 ft above the deck.
  3. Cut the walls from 2 in thermocol: back wall 4 ft × 1.5 ft, side walls 2 ft × 1.5 ft. Glue them to the tray and leave the front open.
  4. Make the flat roof slab 4 × 2 ft with a 3 in parapet and fit it. The whole room must weigh under 8 kg.
  5. Plaster and whitewash with plain flat roofs (no white frosting). Cut the outside stone steps: 8 steps, each 5 in high and about 6 in deep.
  6. Cut the three lamp holes and leave a 1 ft cable tail at each.
  7. Dress the room: mats, blankets, jars, six sleepers. Add the hidden details: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.
  8. Check the room lifts off the hill as one piece and passes the church door.

Before you start

  • Take the cave arch height (2.5 ft) and the +3.3 ft hill top from block B.
  • Gather six 1:6 sleeping travellers (about 1 ft long) or clay to make them.

Thermocol 2 in, 8 × 4 ft (2 sheets), Plywood 12 mm floor tray, 4 × 2 ft (1 piece), PoP + whitewash paint (1 bag + 1 L), Sleeping travellers 1:6 (clay + cloth, or bought) (6), Props: jars, baskets, mats, blankets (lot), Balsa / bamboo for beams, door (lot)

Tools: Hot knife, hobby saw, glue gun, small brushes
