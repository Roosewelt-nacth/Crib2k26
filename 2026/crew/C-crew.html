In short

  1. Cut walls from 2 in thermocol on a 4 × 2 ft base; remove the front wall (cut open).
  2. Make the floor tray from 12 mm ply, resting on two posts and the hill.
  3. Plaster and whitewash; cut stone steps down one side.
  4. Dress the room: mats, blankets, jars, six sleepers, three flicker lamps.
  5. Add hidden details: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.

Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Check the cave arch (2.5 ft) with the mock-up (see block B). STACK NOTE (cave, hill, house): the pack gives the house floor 2.8 ft above the cave floor and the room 1.5 ft high, so the room top is 4.3 ft above the cave floor. Make a cardboard mock-up of cave + house first, stand it on the frame ramp and look at it from the public spot before cutting thermocol.
  2. Cut the 12 mm plywood floor tray 4 × 2 ft. It rests on the hill top and on two 2 × 2 in posts that stand on the cave base. Cut the posts so the floor is 2.8 ft above the cave floor.
  3. Cut the walls from 2 in thermocol: back wall 4 ft × 1.5 ft, side walls 2 ft × 1.5 ft. Glue them to the tray. Leave the front open (cut away).
  4. Make the flat roof slab 4 × 2 ft with a 3 in parapet; fit it on top. Whole room must weigh under 8 kg.
  5. Plaster and whitewash. Cut stone steps down one side: 8 steps, each about 4.2 in high, about 6 in deep.
  6. Dress the room: mats, blankets, jars, six sleepers, three flicker lamps wired together on one 3 V line.
  7. Add the hidden details: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.
  8. Run the lamp wires down through the hill to hatch A. Check the room lifts off the cave as one piece.

Before you start

  • Take the cave arch height (2.5 ft) from block B; the ply floor tray must clear it.
  • Gather six 1:6 sleeping travellers (about 1 ft long) or clay to make them.
