Do exactly this

  2. Make nine small angels the same way, 8–10 in tall, each with a wire stake 8–12 in long.

  5. Check they stand firmly, look obviously handmade and craft-like, and are all white and silver.
  6. Check the clip lamp from block H lights them softly from above.

Every object, one by one: size, materials, how to make it

1. Small angel (make 1 pattern, then 8 more)

Box: 0.4 ft (5 in) W × 0.2 ft (2 in) D × 0.8 ft (10 in) H

Size and place: 8–10 in tall, on a wire stake 8–12 in long; 9 in total.

Materials: 3 white chenille stems, 1 in wooden bead, tulle 6 × 6 in, 2 mm wire 10 in for wings, glue gun.

Reuse: Store in a box with tissue; reuse every year.

How to make it:

  1. Fold one chenille stem in half; twist 2 in from the fold into a head loop and body.
  2. Twist a second stem across as arms.
  3. Push the bead onto the head loop; glue.
  4. Gather tulle into a 3 in skirt; glue at the waist.
  5. Bend 2 mm wire into two 2 in wing loops; cover with tulle; glue behind the shoulders.
  6. Twist a stake wire 8–12 in long onto the bottom.

2. Hero angel

Box: 1 ft (12 in) W × 0.5 ft (6 in) D × 2 ft (24 in) H

Size and place: About 2 ft tall on a 1 ft wire pole at x 1.6, y 2.4 (upper level); wings 12 in wide; halo 3 in.

Materials: 3 mm wire 3 ft (core), 20 chenille stems, 1 in bead, tulle 1 m, 2 mm wire for wings, silver wire ring, cool-white battery micro-LED string.

Reuse: Reuse every year; keep the battery string separate.

How to make it:

  1. Bend the 3 mm wire into a body 2 ft tall with a loop for the head; leave 1 ft extra below as the pole.
  2. Wrap the body in white chenille.
  3. Make a tulle cone robe 10 in across at the hem; glue on.
  4. Make wings from two 6 in wire loops; cover with tulle; glue on.
  5. Add the bead head and a 3 in silver halo; wrap the LED string inside the robe.

3. Placing the choir

Box: 2.4 ft (29 in) W × 3 ft (36 in) D × 2.5 ft (30 in) H

Size and place: Upper level above the sheepfold, x 0.4–2.8, y 1.0–4.0; small angels 8–24 in above the upper level.

Materials: Bamboo sticks for extra height if needed.

How to make it:

  1. Agree the stake holes with block E.
  2. Push the hero pole in at x 1.6, y 2.4.
  3. Push the 9 small angels in around it at different heights, like a choir.
  4. Check all white and silver, no gold, and the clip lamp (block H) lights them from above.

Exact build: do this, in this order

Do exactly this

  1. Confirm the church socket behind the stage (exact spot and circuit rating) and collect the light-point list from every block.
  2. Bench build in the workshop: mount the MCB, RCCB, three switches, fuse block and the three power supplies in the box; label the rails R1, R2, R3 and the switches Lamps, Night sky, Glory.
  3. Bench test every light for 10 seconds on its own switch and check its colour against the list (warm gold, warm white, cool white, indigo).
  4. Cut and label the four trunks T1–T4 and the branch T1b to length; label both ends of every cable.
  5. Install the box at hatch A (back-left of the upper level) and run T1 left, T1b to the field junction, T2 along the back wall and up behind the drape to the ceiling feed, T3 down the right wall, T4 under the hill to the cave.
  6. Fit the warm lights (cave, manger spot, cross-shadow lamp, house, windows, campfire, path, lanterns) into the holes the other blocks left, with the dimmer knob on the box for the cave.
  7. Place the floods on their small stands (three wash floods on the upper level aimed up at the backdrop, two field floods) and clip the angel lamp to the sheepfold post; fit the night-blue gel.
  8. Hang the star burst light and the spiral strings from the ceiling feed with block I; tuck the star points behind the drape pockets from block J; fit the pond lights; set the speaker in hatch C.
  9. An electrician checks the box, earthing and RCCB before the first full run. There is no mains voltage inside the crib.
  10. Rehearse the run with one volunteer: Lamps low (dusk), Night sky on (night), cave dimmer up and Glory on for 20–30 s, all warm, then Night sky off and loop.

Before you start

  • Confirm the church socket behind the stage: its exact spot and circuit rating.
  • Collect the light-point list from every block (hole + 1 ft cable tail at each).
  • Colour rule: gold only in the cave, star, path, lamps and Magi lanterns. No red, bright green, pink, multicolour, flashing or UV lights.
