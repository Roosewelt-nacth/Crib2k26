Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 5 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Confirm there is clear church floor 6 ft to the left of the stage edge and 3 ft left of V, and that the aisle stays 4 ft clear.
  2. Pond: build the plywood tray 1.6 × 3 ft with walls 1.5 in high, line it with blue cloth and cover it with the clear sheet.
  3. Set the pond on the deck at x 0.35–1.95, y 10.3–13.3 with its long side front to back. Edge it with pebbles and sand and add a few artificial reeds.
  4. Leave two light holes for block H (cool-white pond lights) with a 1 ft cable tail. No mains voltage near the pond.
  5. Mirror: build the frame 2 ft × 5 ft from 2 × 2 in battens with a 12 mm ply back; fix the acrylic mirror sheet and round and cover every edge.
  6. Fit a weighted base about 2.5 ft × 1.5 ft with rubber feet so the mirror cannot tip.
  7. Stand the mirror on the floor at x −6, y 19 and turn it about 23° toward the crib. Test with a phone: a person at V with their back to the crib should see the crib behind them. Adjust by up to 10° on the day.
  8. Place the sign and a floor marker for the photo spot; walk through with six people: the queue must not block the view of the crib from V.

Before you start

  • Check with the church that a floor mirror is allowed.
  • Costs are estimates; get quotes before buying the mirror sheet.

Materials to get

Acrylic mirror sheet 2 × 5 ft (shatter-safe, not glass) (1), Mirror frame (2 × 2 in + 12 mm ply back) and weighted base (1 set), Pond tray (12 mm ply 1.6 × 3 ft), blue cloth, clear 3 mm sheet (1), Pebbles, sand, artificial reeds (lot), Sign "Take a photo, share it" + floor marker (lot)

Tools: Saw, drill, staple gun, spirit level, phone for the angle test

Done when (tick each)

  • A person at V sees the crib behind them in the mirror.
  • The mirror and its queue do not block the view from V; 4 ft aisle clear.

Every object, one by one: size, materials, how to make it

Page 5 of 5 · objects

1. Pond tray

Size and place: 1.6 ft wide × 3 ft long, walls 1.5 in high; x 0.35–1.95, y 10.3–13.3, long side front to back. NO water.

Materials: 12 mm ply offcut, 1 × 1½ in strips, blue cloth, clear 3 mm acrylic sheet 1.6 × 3 ft.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the base and screw strips round the edge.
  2. Lay blue cloth inside; wrinkle slightly for ripples.
  3. Lay the clear sheet on top.
  4. Edge with pebbles, sand and 3–5 reeds; leave 2 light holes for block H.

2. Selfie mirror

Size and place: 2 ft × 5 ft acrylic mirror; frame 1.5 in thick; on the church floor at x −6, y 19, turned 23° toward the crib.

Materials: Acrylic mirror 2 × 5 ft, 2 × 2 in battens, 12 mm ply back, weighted base 2.5 × 1.5 ft, rubber feet.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Build the frame and screw the ply back.
  2. Stick the acrylic mirror on; cover every edge.
  3. Screw on the weighted base; check it cannot tip.
  4. Place and turn 23°; test with a phone from V.

3. Sign and marker

Size and place: "Take a photo, share it" sign A4; floor marker at the photo spot.

Materials: Printed A4, stand, floor tape.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Place the sign beside the mirror; tape the marker; keep the aisle 4 ft clear.
