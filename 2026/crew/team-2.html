Exact build: do this, in this order

Do exactly this

  1. Check the cave arch (2.5 ft) and the +3.3 ft hill top with the mock-up (see block B). STACK (cave, hill, house): the cave floor is at deck level and the arch is 2.5 ft high. The hill top and the house floor are at +3.3 ft, so about 10 in of rock sits above the arch. The room is 1.5 ft high, so its roof is at +4.8 ft. Make a cardboard mock-up of cave + house first and look at it from the public spot before cutting the hill or the wood.
  2. Cut the 9 mm plywood floor tray 4 × 2 ft and screw 1 × 1 in strips under its edges. It rests on the hill top at +3.3 ft and on two 2 × 2 in posts that stand on the cave base; cut the posts so the floor is exactly 3.3 ft above the deck.
  3. Cut the walls from 4 mm plywood: back wall 4 ft × 1.5 ft, side walls 2 ft × 1.5 ft. Glue and screw them to the tray and to 1 × 1 in corner strips; leave the front open.
  4. Cut the roof slab 4 × 2 ft from 4 mm plywood, add a 3 in parapet strip on three sides and screw it on (no glue, so it lifts off for the lamps). The whole room must weigh under 12 kg.
  5. Fill screw heads and joints with wall putty, then brush on putty mixed with fine sand for a stone texture; whitewash; roofs plain flat (no white frosting). Make the 8 stair blocks (5 in rise, 6 in tread) from offcuts.
  6. Drill the three 10 mm lamp holes in the roof slab and leave a 1 ft cable tail at each.
  7. Dress the room: mats, blankets, jars, six sleepers. Add the hidden details: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.
  8. Check the room lifts off the hill as one piece and passes the church door. After Christmas unscrew the roof and store the room flat for next year.

Before you start

  • Take the cave arch height (2.5 ft) and the +3.3 ft hill top from block B.
  • Gather six 1:6 sleeping travellers (about 1 ft long) or clay to make them.

1. Floor tray

Box: 4 ft (48 in) W × 2 ft (24 in) D × 0.1 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: 4 ft × 2 ft, 9 mm plywood; floor at +3.3 ft; turned 33° toward V on the hill top (x 3.0–7.4, y 4.2–8.1).

Materials: 9 mm plywood 4 × 2 ft, 1 × 1 in pine strip 12 ft, 1 in screws, wood glue.

Reuse: Screwed wood room comes apart and is reused every year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the plywood 4 × 2 ft with straight square corners (check the diagonals are equal).
  2. Screw 1 × 1 in strips under all four edges as a stiff frame.
  3. Sand the edges smooth.
  4. Mark the open front side "FRONT (to V)".

2. Two posts

Box: 0.17 ft (2 in) W × 0.17 ft (2 in) D × 3.3 ft (40 in) H

Size and place: 2 × 2 in, each 40 in minus 9 mm (about 39⅝ in); stand on the cave base, not on the cave roof.

Materials: 2 × 2 in pine 7 ft, 2 small L-brackets.

Reuse: Reusable.

How to make it:

  1. Place the tray on the hill top with the cardboard mock-up first and mark where the posts land on the cave base.
  2. Cut the posts so the floor is exactly 3.3 ft above the deck.
  3. Screw each post to the underside frame of the tray with an L-bracket.
  4. Check with a level: the floor is flat both ways.

3. Back wall and side walls

Box: 4 ft (48 in) W × 2 ft (24 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: Back wall 4 ft × 1.5 ft high; two side walls 2 ft × 1.5 ft high; all 4 mm plywood; front open.

Materials: 4 mm plywood, 4 corner strips 1 × 1 in × 1.5 ft, ¾ in screws, wood glue.

Reuse: Screw everything so it comes apart.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the three wall pieces; sand the edges.
  2. Cut a small window 4 × 3 in in the back wall 6 in from one end, 9 in up.
  3. Glue and screw a corner strip to the inside back corners and the front corners.
  4. Screw the walls to the tray edge and to the corner strips, a screw every 6 in.
  5. Check the walls stand square (90°) to the floor.

4. Roof slab with parapet

Box: 4 ft (48 in) W × 2 ft (24 in) D × 0.25 ft (3 in) H

Size and place: 4 ft × 2 ft, 4 mm plywood, at +4.8 ft; parapet 3 in high on the back and two sides.

Materials: 4 mm plywood 4 × 2 ft, 1 × 3 in pine strip 8 ft.

Reuse: Screws only, no glue, so it lifts off for the lamps.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the slab; drill three 10 mm lamp holes 1 ft apart along the middle.
  2. Screw the 1 × 3 in parapet strips along the back and both short sides.
  3. Screw the slab onto the top edge of the walls (4 screws).
  4. Weigh the whole room: under 12 kg.

5. Stone texture and whitewash

Box: 4 ft (48 in) W × 2 ft (24 in) D × 1.5 ft (18 in) H

Size and place: All outside walls and parapet; roof top plain flat.

Materials: Wall putty + fine sand (1 : 1), whitewash or white emulsion, sand-colour paint, brushes.

Reuse: The texture hides the wood so the room looks like stone for years.

How to make it:

  1. Fill screw heads and joints with putty; let dry; sand smooth.
  2. Mix putty with sand and dab it on with a stiff brush for a rough stone look.
  3. Whitewash; dry-brush sand colour on the edges and low parts.
  4. Leave the roof plain and flat (no white frosting, no snow).

6. Outside stair

Box: 1 ft (12 in) W × 4 ft (48 in) D × 3.3 ft (40 in) H

Size and place: 8 steps, each 5 in high, 6 in deep, 1 ft wide; total rise 3.3 ft (40 in); runs up the hill side to the room.

Materials: Wood offcuts 5 in thick (or stacked pieces), screws, the same putty texture.

Reuse: Use offcuts only.

How to make it:

  1. Cut 8 blocks 1 ft wide × 6 in deep × 5 in high from offcuts; stack each block 6 in further back than the one below.
  2. Screw the blocks together into one staircase piece.
  3. Texture with putty + sand and paint limestone.
  4. Press it into the hill side so the top step meets the room floor.
