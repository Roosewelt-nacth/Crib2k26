In short

  1. Confirm deck height, size and load rating on site; mark the 9 × 16 ft grid.

Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 4 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. On site: measure the real deck. The plan assumes it is 2 ft high and a free 9 × 16 ft. Mark the 9 × 16 ft rectangle with tape and tick every 4 ft along the front and the left side.
  2. Workshop: lay out 2 × 2 in battens in 4 × 4 ft squares. Fix the 4 sides with screws (not glue), then add one cross batten each way at the 2 ft centre. Make 8 squares and 4 strips of 1 × 4 ft. Number each section in marker.
  3. Cut legs to the heights in the cut list (front part flat, then the ramp, then 3.0 ft at the back). Screw legs to the section corners and mid-points under the ramp and back area.
  4. Cross-brace every leg taller than 1.5 ft with diagonal battens in both directions.
  5. Skin the tops with 9–12 mm plywood, screws about every 6 in at the edges and 12 in inside. Cut and leave the three hatch lids loose (sizes and places in the cut list).
  6. Fix the black skirting with Velcro along the front edge (9 ft) and the left edge (16 ft), 2 ft drop, so the open sides are hidden to the floor.
  7. Spray all cloth and any thermocol with fire retardant (4 L in total).
  8. Dry-fit the whole 9 × 16 ft frame in the workshop on a taped outline. Stand two people on each section: it must not flex. Check every section passes through the church door.

Before you start

  • Measure the real deck height, size and load rating; the leg heights assume a 2 ft deck.
  • Mark the 9 × 16 ft grid on the deck with tape.
  • Number every 4 × 4 ft frame section so it can be put back in the same place.

Materials to get

Pine/casuarina battens 2 × 2 in (~160 ft), Plywood 9–12 mm, 8 × 4 ft (5 sheets), Screws, nails, brackets, wood glue (lot), Black pleated skirting + Velcro (25 ft run × 2 ft), Fire-retardant spray (all cloth, thermocol, cotton) (4 L)

Tools: Saw, drill, screw gun, spirit level, measuring tape, staple gun

  • Service hatches A (control box), B (angel and mister) and C (speaker) open freely and are labelled.
  • Skirting on the open front and left edges hides the deck completely.
  • Every section passes through the church door.
