Do exactly this

  1. Agree the ridge position with block F (floating angel): the ridge sits at y 4.6–5.6 in the same zone.
  2. Build four plywood boxes 3.4 ft wide × 1.75 ft deep with tops at 0.8, 1.2, 1.6 and 2.0 ft. Screw each to block A in order from the back wall (tallest at the back).
  3. Face the box fronts with 2 in thermocol carved as dry-stone walls; glue real pebbles on the fronts.
  4. Cover the treads with soil or sand, then patches of artificial grass and moss. Paint olive treads, sand paths, limestone walls.
  5. Build the sheepfold (pebble ring 2.0 × 1.3 ft, thorn gate) at x 0.4–2.4, y 1.75–3.05.
  6. Build the watchtower at x 3.0, y 1.0 (1 ft across, 1.5 ft tall) on the +2.0 ft terrace.
  7. Build the shepherd ledge 1.7 × 2.6 ft at +0.8 ft (x 0.2–1.9, y 7.0–9.6). Place the 2 shepherds, 6 sheep and the campfire last.
  8. Wire the campfire LED to hatch A with a 1 ft service loop. Check the boxes lift out one at a time.

Before you start

  • Blocks E and F (floating angel) share this zone; agree the ridge position with block F before fixing the boxes.

Thermocol 4 in ×3 + 2 in ×1, 8 × 4 ft (4 sheets), PoP + grout (2 bags), Soil, sand, artificial grass, moss (lot), Paint + brushes (4 L), Shepherd figures 1.2 ft (old man, young man + lamb) (2), Wool sheep (6), Campfire: twigs + orange flicker LED (1), Pebbles + glue for the sheepfold (lot), Watchtower: PVC pipe, clay, pebbles (1)

Tools: Hot knife, glue gun, brushes, soldering iron
