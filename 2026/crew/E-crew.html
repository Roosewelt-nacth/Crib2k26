Exact build: do this, in this order

Do exactly this

  1. Agree the angel choir place with block F: it stands on the upper level above the sheepfold.
  2. Face the step: glue 2 in thermocol 9 ft long × 1.5 ft high to the step, carve it as a dry-stone wall and glue real pebbles on it.
  3. Upper level: build the sheepfold (pebble ring 2.0 × 1.3 ft with a thorn gate) at x 0.4–2.4, y 1.75–3.05 and the watchtower at x 3.0, y 1.0, 1 ft across and 1.5 ft tall.
  4. Cover the upper and lower ground with sand and soil, then patches of grass and moss. Paint olive, sand and limestone colours.
  5. Lower level: make the campfire from twigs and pebbles at x 1.0, y 8.3 and leave a light hole.
  6. Place the 2 shepherds and the 6 sheep last (shepherds 1.2 ft tall, sheep about 5 in).
  7. Leave holes and 1 ft cable tails for the campfire light and the two field floods.
  8. Check the pieces lift out one at a time.

Before you start

  • Check the angel choir place with block F: the choir stands above the sheepfold.
  • Leave holes and 1 ft cable tails for the campfire light and the two field floods (block H).

Materials to get

Thermocol 2 in, 8 × 4 ft (step wall, sheepfold base) (2 sheets), PoP + grout (1 bag), Soil, sand, artificial grass, moss (lot), Paint + brushes (3 L), Shepherd figures 1.2 ft (old man, young man + lamb) (2), Wool sheep (6), Campfire: twigs and pebbles (light fitted by H) (1), Pebbles + glue for the sheepfold (lot), Watchtower: PVC pipe, clay, pebbles (1)

Tools: Hot knife, glue gun, brushes
