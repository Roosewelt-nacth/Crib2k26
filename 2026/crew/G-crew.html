Do exactly this

  1. Cut house blocks from 2 in thermocol: A 1.4 × 1.6 ft × 2.0 ft, B 1.6 × 1.8 ft × 2.5 ft, C 1.4 × 1.6 ft × 1.8 ft. Cut doors (about 0.9 ft high at 1:6) and windows.
  2. Add wood lintels and the outside stair on house B.
  3. Plaster, lime-wash and stain the cut edges with a thin brown glaze. Walls stay 2 in thick.
  4. Make the street 1.1 × 6.7 ft, sloping 0.3 → 0.8 ft; pave with cut cork or grooved clay; add a drainage channel.
  5. Build the well (0.9 ft across, 0.6 ft high) with a sealed resin pool.
  6. Place the houses against the right wall; keep 1 in clear below the drape (block J) bottom.
  7. Fit the 15 window lamps and run the wires through the base to hatch A.
  8. Place the census scene, donkeys and travellers last (figures 1.2 ft tall, 1:6).

Before you start

  • Keep 1 in clear between house tops and the drape bottom (block J).
  • Gather props: jars, baskets, barrels.
