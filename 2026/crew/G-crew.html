Do exactly this

  1. Build three plywood boxes (6 mm walls on 1 × 1 in corner strips, 12 mm base, screwed): A 1.4 ft wide × 1.6 ft long × 2.0 ft high, B 1.6 × 1.8 × 2.5 ft, C 1.4 × 1.6 × 1.8 ft. The back (wall side) stays open. Cut doors (0.9 ft high × 0.5 ft wide) and windows (4 × 3 in) with a jigsaw.
  2. Add wood lintels and the outside stair on house B.
  3. Fill joints and screw heads with wall putty, brush on putty mixed with fine sand for a stone texture, lime-wash, and glaze the door and window edges thin brown. Roofs plain and flat.
  4. Make the street 1.1 × 6.7 ft, flat, with a cobble pattern in cork or grooved clay.
  5. Build the well (0.9 ft across, 0.6 ft high) with a blue plastic sheet for the water.
  6. Place the houses against the right wall; keep 1 in clear below the drape bottom (block J).
  7. Leave the 15 window holes and a 1 ft cable tail inside each house (block H fits the lamps).
  8. Place the census scene, donkeys and travellers last (figures 1.2 ft tall, 1:6).

1. House A (wood)

Size and place: 1.4 ft wide (across) × 1.6 ft long (along the wall) × 2.0 ft high; x 7.6–9.0, y 9.4–11.0; 5 windows.

Materials: 6 mm plywood, 12 mm base 1.4 × 1.6 ft, 1 × 1 in pine corner strips, ¾ in screws, wood glue.

Reuse: Screwed wood house: store flat and reuse every year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the base 1.4 × 1.6 ft from 12 mm ply.
  2. Cut walls from 6 mm ply: front (facing the street) 1.6 × 2.0 ft, two ends 1.4 × 2.0 ft; the back against the church wall stays open.
  3. Mark a door 0.9 ft high × 0.5 ft wide in the front; mark windows 4 × 3 in; drill a corner hole and cut with a jigsaw.
  4. Glue and screw 1 × 1 in strips in the corners; screw the walls to the base and strips.
  5. Cut a flat roof 1.4 × 1.6 ft; add a 1 in parapet strip on the front and ends; screw on.
  6. Putty the joints, dab putty + sand texture, lime-wash, glaze window and door edges thin brown.

2. House B (wood, outside stair)

Size and place: 1.6 ft wide × 1.8 ft long × 2.5 ft high; x 7.4–9.0, y 11.3–13.1; 6 windows; outside stair.

Materials: As house A, plus 6 offcut blocks for the stair.

Reuse: Reuse every year.

How to make it:

  1. Build exactly as house A with these sizes: base 1.6 × 1.8 ft, front 1.8 × 2.5 ft, ends 1.6 × 2.5 ft.
  2. Door 0.9 × 0.5 ft; 6 windows 4 × 3 in (3 low, 3 high).
  3. Stair: 6 offcut blocks 5 in high × 4 in deep × 4 in wide, stacked up the front wall to the roof; screw.
  4. Add a wooden lintel over the door (1 × ½ in strip).
  5. Texture and lime-wash as house A.

3. House C (wood)

Size and place: 1.4 ft wide × 1.6 ft long × 1.8 ft high; x 7.6–9.0, y 13.4–15.0; 4 windows.

Materials: As house A.

Reuse: Reuse every year.

How to make it:

  1. Build as house A: base 1.4 × 1.6 ft, front 1.6 × 1.8 ft, ends 1.4 × 1.8 ft.
  2. Door 0.9 × 0.5 ft; 4 windows 4 × 3 in.
  3. Texture and lime-wash as house A; keep it slightly darker than A and B.

4. Cobbled street

Size and place: 1.1 ft wide × 6.7 ft long, flat at deck level; x 6.2–7.3, y 9.3–16.0.

Materials: 3 mm hardboard or ply offcuts in 2 pieces, cork sheet or air-dry clay, sand paint.

Reuse: Make it on boards so it lifts out and is reused.

How to make it:

  1. Cut two boards 1.1 × 3.35 ft.
  2. Glue cork pieces about 1 × 1 in, or press grooves into a thin clay layer.
  3. Paint sand, dry-brush limestone on top of the cobbles.
  4. Lay the boards end to end on the deck.

5. Well

Size and place: 11 in across, 7 in high; centre x 5.6, y 11.2.

Materials: PVC pipe 10 in (or a plastic tub) cut 7 in high, clay, pebbles, blue plastic sheet, a stick frame and string.

Reuse: Reusable; no real water.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the pipe or tub 7 in high.
  2. Cover outside with clay and press pebbles in.
  3. Glue a blue plastic sheet circle 1 in below the rim as water.
  4. Add a small stick frame with a string and a tiny bucket.

6. Census table, soldier and scribe

Size and place: Table 1.4 × 1.0 ft; figures 1.2 ft (1:6); x 4.5–5.9, y 12.8–13.8.

Materials: Wood offcuts for the table, rolled paper scrolls; reuse 2023 figures.

Reuse: Reuse first.

How to make it:

  1. Make the table: top 1.4 × 1.0 ft from 6 mm ply, 4 legs 4 in long.
  2. Roll small paper scrolls; tie with thread; stain with tea.
  3. Seat the scribe behind the table, the soldier standing beside it with a spear.
  4. Add 3 villagers queueing toward the street.
