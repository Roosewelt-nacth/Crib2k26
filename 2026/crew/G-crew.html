Exact build: do this, in this order

Page 4 of 8 · build it

Do exactly this

  1. Build five plywood boxes (6 mm walls on 1 × 1 in corner strips, 12 mm base, screwed) to the sizes in the cut list: 3.5, 3.0, 2.5, 2.0 and 1.5 ft high. The back (wall side) stays open.
  2. Cut doors and windows with a jigsaw (houses 1–2: door 1.2 × 0.6 ft, windows 4 × 3 in; houses 3–5: door 1.0 × 0.5 ft, windows 3 × 2 in).
  3. Add the outside stair on house 2 (7 offcut blocks, 5 in high), wooden lintels and the roof parapets.
  4. Fill joints with wall putty, dab putty + sand for a stone texture, lime-wash; paint houses 4–5 a shade paler and bluer (they are farther away).
  5. Make the street boards 1.25 ft wide at the front narrowing to 0.55 ft at the back, with cobbles that get smaller toward the back.
  6. Build the well (0.9 ft across, 0.6 ft high), the market stall with its cloth awning, and the 3.5 ft palm.
  7. Place the houses against the right wall, tallest at the front; keep 1 in clear below the drape bottom (block J). Leave the 20 window holes and the cable tails.
  8. Place the census scene, donkeys, travellers, chickens, washing line and lamps last (people 1 ft tall). Check from V that the census table and well are visible.

Before you start

  • Leave 20 small window holes (houses 1–5: 6, 5, 4, 3, 2) and a 1 ft cable tail inside each house (block H fits the lamps).
  • Keep 1 in clear below the drape bottom (block J).

Tools: Jigsaw or hand saw, drill + 3 mm bit, screwdriver, sandpaper, putty knife, glue gun, brushes

1. House 1 (front, roof terrace) (wood)

Box: 1.65 ft (20 in) W × 2 ft (24 in) D × 3.5 ft (42 in) H

Size and place: Box 1.65 ft wide (across) × 2.0 ft long (along the wall) × 3.5 ft high; x 7.35–9.0, y 14.0–16.0; 6 windows.

Materials: 6 mm plywood, 12 mm base 1.65 × 2.0 ft, 1 × 1 in pine corner strips, ¾ in screws, wood glue.

Reuse: Screwed wood house: store flat and reuse every year.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the base 1.65 × 2.0 ft from 12 mm ply.
  2. Cut walls from 6 mm ply: front (facing the street) 2.0 × 3.5 ft, two ends 1.65 × 3.5 ft; the back against the church wall stays open.
  3. Mark the door 1.2 × 0.6 ft and 6 windows; drill a corner hole and cut with a jigsaw.
  4. Glue and screw 1 × 1 in strips in the corners; screw the walls to the base and strips.
  5. Cut a flat roof 1.65 × 2.0 ft; screw a 1 in parapet strip on the front and ends. Add a tiny ladder and a rug on the roof terrace.
  6. Putty the joints, dab putty + sand texture, lime-wash; it is the nearest house, so give it the richest detail.

6. Cobbled street (narrows away from you)

Box: 1.25 ft (15 in) W × 6.8 ft (82 in) D × 0.05 ft (1 in) H

Size and place: Flat at deck level: 1.25 ft wide at the front (x 6.0–7.25, y 16) narrowing to 0.55 ft at the back (x 7.0–7.55, y 9.2); 6.8 ft long.

Materials: 3 mm hardboard or ply offcuts in 2 pieces, cork sheet or air-dry clay, sand paint.

Reuse: Make it on boards so it lifts out and is reused.

How to make it:

  1. Draw the tapering street on two boards (front board 1.25 → 0.9 ft wide, back board 0.9 → 0.55 ft).
  2. Cut the boards; lay them end to end to check the taper is smooth.
  3. Glue cobbles: 1 × 1 in at the front, getting smaller to ½ × ½ in at the back.
  4. Paint sand, dry-brush limestone on the cobbles.

7. Well

Box: 0.9 ft (11 in) W × 0.9 ft (11 in) D × 0.6 ft (7 in) H

Size and place: 11 in across, 7 in high; centre x 5.6, y 11.2.

Materials: PVC pipe 10 in (or a plastic tub) cut 7 in high, clay, pebbles, blue plastic sheet, a stick frame and string.

Reuse: Reusable; no real water.

How to make it:

  1. Cut the pipe or tub 7 in high.
  2. Cover outside with clay and press pebbles in.
  3. Glue a blue plastic sheet circle 1 in below the rim as water.
  4. Add a small stick frame with a string and a tiny bucket.
