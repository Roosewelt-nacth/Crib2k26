1. The Book of Bethlehem

The stage is a giant open storybook. Six pop-up scenes stand on its pages and light up one after another as a narrator reads; the star slides across the top and stops over the Nativity.

Book
14 × 7 ft, curved plywood base, pages 6 in thick at the edge
Scenes
five at 2–2.3 ft wide, Nativity spread 3 × 3 ft
Heights
scene cut-outs 2–3 ft; Nativity 3.5 ft
Moves
6 light channels + star on a pulley, 4-min audio

2. Room in Our House

An apartment block with its front wall removed. Every flat shows a real household on Christmas Eve. One by one the lights go out, until only the ground-floor parking is glowing, where Jesus is born.

Block
12 ft W × 6 ft H × 2.5 ft D
Flats
9 rooms, each 4 × 1.4 ft
Ground floor
1.8 ft high parking, Nativity 3.4 ft wide
Moves
10 light channels; one family figure slides out to the parking
Option 3

3. Born Among the Fishermen

A Tamil Nadu fishing village on Christmas night. Catamarans on the sand, nets drying, fisher-folk as the shepherds. The lighthouse beam sweeps the hall, then stops on the thatched hut.

Sea
full 16 ft, 2.6 ft deep, 3 rolling wave layers
Hut
4 × 2.4 ft, 2.5 ft high
Lighthouse
1.2 ft base, 5 ft high, rotating beam
Moves
wave motor, beam motor, sound of waves
Option 4

4. The Long Road

Mary and Joseph's journey told in black silhouettes against a sky that turns from sunset to night. Only the stable window is lit in colour. The travellers move slowly across the hills toward it.

Sky screen
16 × 7 ft frosted sheet at the back
Layers
4 cut-out layers, 2–2.5 ft high, spaced 1.5–2 ft
Stable
3.8 × 2.6 ft at front right
Moves
RGB sky fade, travellers on a hidden track
Option 5

5. All Creation Rejoices

Everything is made from waste the parish collects in November. A grotto of hundreds of lit bottles slowly changes colour, with newspaper animals and a star made from bottle bottoms.

Grotto
8 ft W × 6 ft H arch on wire mesh
Bottles
≈ 600 × 1 L, LED strip inside the rows
Nativity
3.4 × 1.8 ft inside the grotto
Moves
slow colour cycle; cards say what each piece was
