# Crib 2026: Ideas and Image Prompts

**Stage:** 16 ft wide × 9 ft deep, platform with black skirting, backdrop about 7 ft above it.
**Holy Family:** about 2 ft tall (0.7 ft infant).
**Timeline:** decide by 10 Nov · build 11 Nov – 6 Dec · details 7–15 Dec · assemble 16–20 Dec · rehearse 21–22 Dec.

## How to use the prompts

- Paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Adobe Firefly. Attach your 2023 crib photo and add "same hall, same style of photo" for a closer match.
- If it looks like a digital illustration, add: *"not an illustration, not CGI, a real physical handmade model photographed."*
- If the figures come out the wrong size, add: *"the statues are the largest figures; buildings are miniature."*
- Every prompt ends with the same camera line so all the images are comparable.

### 23. Desert Caravan

Real sand dunes, a moving caravan, and a giant hourglass with flowing sand counting down to the birth.
**Key sizes:** dunes over the full stage, up to 2 ft high; hourglass 4 ft tall (two clear bottles or acrylic cones on a stand); stable 3.5 × 2.5 ft. **Moving part:** caravan on a hidden track, sand flowing in the hourglass.

```
A real photograph, taken on a phone, of a handmade Christmas crib inside an Indian church hall at night, 16 feet wide and 9 feet deep, on a raised platform with black pleated cloth skirting. The platform is covered in real golden sand shaped into rolling desert dunes, lit warm amber from the side so the ripples cast soft shadows. A caravan of the three wise men on camels with richly coloured saddle cloths and lanterns crosses the dunes toward the right. On the left stands a tall ornate wooden hourglass about 4 feet high with glowing sand flowing through it. On the right, a small palm oasis and a stone-and-timber stable holds real resin statues of Mary, Joseph and baby Jesus (2 feet tall), an ox and a donkey, under a bright star whose beam reaches down to the manger. Matte deep-blue desert night-sky backdrop with stars. Photographed from 8 feet away at adult eye height, natural phone-camera exposure, dark hall around the display, photorealistic, 16:9.
```

## Effects you can add to any concept

| Effect | How | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|
| Floating angel | An angled clear acrylic sheet (45°) reflects a hidden phone or tablet playing an angel video, so the angel seems to float in mid-air | Low |
| Moving figures | Geared motors (10–30 RPM) with cams and levers to nod heads, rock the cradle or walk figures on a track | Low |
| Turntable | Lazy-Susan bearing under a plywood disc, 1 RPM motor | Medium |
| Fog / mist | Small fog machine, or an ultrasonic mister in water | Low–medium |
| Kids' button | Arduino with an arcade button that starts the light show | Low |
| Narration | DFPlayer mini with a speaker; record a 3–4 min story with carols | Low |
| Projection | One projector adds moving sky, snow or rain to any backdrop | Medium |
