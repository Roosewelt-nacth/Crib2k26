# Craft plan: replace mechanics with low-budget craft props (proposal, 19 Nov 2026)

Goal: no screens, relays, microcontrollers, mist, resin or heavy rigging. Everything is plywood, thermocol, cloth, paper, wire, pipe cleaners and battery lights. Costs below are estimates for the lean tier; confirm with quotes.

Rough effect: about ₹25,000 to ₹35,000 less on the lean tier, no electrician beyond one safety check of the extension board, and Team 3's load drops a lot (H, I, F and J all get simpler).

## What you lose
- The synchronised 4-minute show. It becomes three light scenes switched by hand by one volunteer.
- The path-of-light chase, mist, the hologram angel and the twinkling fibre stars.
- What stays: cave glow, star glow, lamps in the windows, the angel choir, the cloud spiral, the Magi lanterns.

## Block F as pipe-cleaner angels ("Glory choir")
- **Hero angel:** about 2 ft tall. Body and arms from thick chenille stems twisted over a wire core; head a 1 in wooden bead; robe of white tulle or tissue; wings a wire loop filled with coffee-filter petals or tulle; halo white or silver, not gold. A battery fairy-light string is wrapped inside the robe.
- **Small angels:** 8 to 10 angels, 8 to 10 in tall, same method.
- **Where:** on the upper layer above the step. The hero angel sits on a wire pole about 3 ft above the sheepfold, and the small angels are on wire stakes pushed into the rock and sheepfold stones at different heights. No ceiling rigging.
- **Light:** one cool-white clip lamp comes on in the Glory moment.
- **Budget:** pipe cleaners, tulle, beads, wire and fairy lights come to about ₹1,000. It takes about 2 person-days and suits helpers.

## Still needs a plug (kept deliberately small)
One extension board with three switches, checked once by an electrician; no mains voltage inside the crib; the floodlights, rope light and speaker plug in; everything else is battery or USB.
