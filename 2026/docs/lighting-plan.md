# Lighting plan v2 (19 Nov 2026): one inlet, one box, Austin's team

Nothing in the guides is rebuilt yet. This is the plan to approve first. Drawing: `2026/images/lighting-plan.png` (made by `source/make_lighting.py`).

Your answers are built in: the church socket is right behind the stage in the back-left corner; wash floods are kept as simple as possible (no ceiling mounting); the Lighting job stays with Austin Joel, Richard and Allwyn; no UV bars and no optional extras.

## 1. One port for the whole crib
- **One 230 V plug** from the church socket behind the stage (back-left corner, x 0, y 0) into **one distribution box (D)** at the back-left corner of the upper layer (x 0–1.2, y 0.2–1.0, under a loose lid). The plug lead is about 1 m.
- The box holds a 6 A MCB, a 30 mA RCCB, three scene switches, a fuse block and the power supplies. The expected load is about 215 W, about 1 A, so the socket needs no special rating (confirm the socket and its circuit on site).
- Everything inside the crib is **12 V or 5 V**. No mains voltage behind the rock or the drape.
- Two 12 V 10 A supplies (R1 warm lamps, R2 floods and wash) and one 5 V 5 A supply (R3 USB lights and speaker). Each has its own fuse.
- Hatch A (control box) therefore moves from the front-right corner to this back-left corner.

## 4. Three switches, worked by hand
- **S1 LAMPS** (R1): cave, spots, house, windows, campfire, path, star burst, spiral, lanterns, pond. The cave dimmer is a knob on the box.
- **S2 NIGHT SKY** (R2 + R3): indigo wash, star points, sound.
- **S3 GLORY** (R2): field floods and the angel lamp.
- Suggested run, about 4 minutes, repeated by one volunteer: S1 low (dusk) → S2 on (night) → cave dimmer up, S3 on for 20–30 s (Glory) then off → all warm → S2 off and loop.
