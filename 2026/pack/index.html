Composition rules

  1. Focal point dead centre: the cave and star. Keep the rock around the cave one shade darker so the gold glows.
  2. Leading lines: the path of light and the cascade spiral both point at the manger.
  3. Balance: cool white-blue angel and field on the left, many small warm lamps on the right, each dimmer than the cave.
  4. Atmospheric depth: pieces get paler, cooler and less saturated with distance (tiers A to D).
  5. Frame: dark, low-saturation olive trees in the two front corners.
  6. Repeat in threes: three lamps, three kings, three terrace steps.
  7. Gold in five places only: cave, star, path, lamps, Magi lanterns. A sixth glowing gold thing comes out.
  8. Proportion: about 60% cool blue, 30% earth, 10% gold.
  9. Not allowed: Christmas red, bright green, pink, primaries, colour-cycling or strobing lights.

How to build it

  1. Confirm deck height, size and load rating on site; mark the 9 × 16 ft grid.
  2. Prefab the frame in 4 × 4 ft sections at the workshop; label each section.
  3. Cut legs to the heights on the plan; screw to the battens at 2 ft centres.

How to build it

  1. Glue 4 in thermocol into a block (hill ≈ 7 × 6 × 3 ft); cut the 4 × 2.5 ft cave inside it.
  2. Carve strata and cracks with a hot knife; cut the arch 3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high.
  3. Mesh the arch and overhangs; coat with PoP + PVA, 3 mm.
  4. Sand/grout coat while wet; press in real pebbles and dry grass.
  5. Dry-brush sand, then ochre, then olive; darker in cracks.
  6. Fit manger, straw, animals and figures last; hide the LED above the lintel.
  7. Split into cave and hill pieces that pass through the church door.
