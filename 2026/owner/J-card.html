What to do, and when

  1. 20–22 NovMeasure the real ceiling height: the drape length and cost depend on it.
  2. 23–26 NovFit the ceiling track and a rope backup along both walls first.
  3. 27–29 NovHang the 5 print panels, each 5 × 5 ft, and tape the joins flat. The corner is rounded with a 1.5 ft radius.
  4. 30 Nov – 3 DecSew and pleat the drape at 1.5× fullness and hang it on rings so its hem laps the print by 6 in.
  5. 4–6 DecSew a pocket along the top of the print on both walls for the star lights (block H fits them).
  6. 7–10 DecGlue the gold stars in irregular clusters.
  7. 11–13 DecCut and paint the 12 choir angels (2 ft tall) in white and silver and fix them to the drape.
  8. 14–17 DecCheck from the public spot: the print joins are flat, the pleats are even, and no colour is used outside the palette.
