  1. 23–25 NovCut a 14 mm groove along the path; fit the channel flush.
  2. 26–29 NovSolder the strip (5 V, GND, data) and seal with heat-shrink.
  3. 30 Nov – 3 DecFix the diffuser strip over the LEDs.
  4. 4–7 DecGlue fine sand on top and pebbles at the edges.
  5. 8–10 DecRun data to hatch A; test the chase at 20 px/s.
  6. 11–14 DecWire everything on a bench board first; test each channel for 10 s.
  7. 15–18 DecSeparate 12 V and 5 V rails, one fuse each, one common ground.
  8. 19–22 DecLabel every cable at both ends; leave a 1 ft service loop at each hatch.
