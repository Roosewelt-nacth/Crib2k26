How to build it, in order

  1. Cut walls from 2 in thermocol on a 4 × 2 ft base; remove the front wall (cut open).
  2. Make the floor tray from 12 mm ply, resting on two posts and the hill.
  3. Plaster and whitewash; cut stone steps down one side.
  4. Dress the room: mats, blankets, jars, six sleepers, three flicker lamps.
  5. Add hidden details: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.

Before you start

  • Take the cave arch height (2.5 ft) from block B; the ply floor tray must clear it.
  • Gather six 1:6 sleeping travellers (about 1 ft long) or clay to make them.

Check before cutting

Floor at +2.8 ft must clear the cave arch (2.5 ft) with the ply tray.

Done when (tick each)

  • Whole room weighs under 8 kg and sits on the posts, not on the cave roof.
  • Three flicker lamps work on one 3 V line.
  • Hidden details placed: cat on the roof, clay oven, sleeping child.
  • Lifts off the cave as one piece.
