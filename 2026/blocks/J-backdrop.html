How to build it, in order

  1. Fit the ceiling track and a rope backup along both walls.
  2. Hang the five print panels; tape the joins flat.
  3. Sew and pleat the drape (1.5× fullness); hang on rings.
  4. Push 250 fibre ends through the top band; tape and route to the engine.
  5. Glue gold stars in irregular clusters.
  6. Paint 12 angels with UV paint; fit UV bars behind the print lip and test in the dark.

Before you start

  • Order the print and drape early; they are long-lead items.
  • Measure the real ceiling height; drape cost scales with it (about ₹280 per extra foot of drop).

Check before cutting

Swing item: drape cost scales with ceiling height (add about ₹280 per extra foot of drop).

Done when (tick each)

  • Track and rope backup are fitted; print joins are flat and tape-free from V.
  • Drape pleats are even at 1.5× fullness and the hem laps the print by 6 in.
  • UV angels are invisible with lights on and glow violet in the dark.
  • Fibre ends twinkle without hotspots.
