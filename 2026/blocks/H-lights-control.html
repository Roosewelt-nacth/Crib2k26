How to build it, in order

  1. Path Of Light
  2. Cut a 14 mm groove along the path; fit the channel flush.
  3. Solder the strip (5 V, GND, data) and seal with heat-shrink.
  4. Fix the diffuser strip over the LEDs.
  5. Glue fine sand on top and pebbles at the edges.
  6. Run data to hatch A; test the chase at 20 px/s.
  7. Show Control
  8. Wire everything on a bench board first; test each channel for 10 s.
  9. Separate 12 V and 5 V rails, one fuse each, one common ground.
  10. Label every cable at both ends; leave a 1 ft service loop at each hatch.
  11. Program the 4-minute cue sheet; add a start button and manual override.
  12. Set the audio to 60 dB or less at the view spot.
  13. An electrician checks earthing and the RCCB before the first full run.

Before you start

  • Wire everything on a bench board first; test every channel for 10 seconds.
  • Confirm the church socket and its circuit rating.

Check before cutting

The path must never cross a solid object; it is checked on the master plan.

Check before cutting

No mains voltage inside the crib. The church socket and its circuit rating must be confirmed.

Done when (tick each)

  • Every channel cues correctly on the 4-minute sheet; the loop restarts at 3:55.
  • Path chase runs front edge to cave mouth in 7 s and never crosses a solid.
  • Audio at 60 dB or less at V.
  • An electrician has checked earthing and the RCCB; no mains voltage inside the crib.
