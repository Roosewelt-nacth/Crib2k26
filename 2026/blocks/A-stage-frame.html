How to build it, in order

  1. Confirm deck height, size and load rating on site; mark the 9 × 16 ft grid.
  2. Prefab the frame in 4 × 4 ft sections at the workshop; label each section.
  3. Cut legs to the heights on the plan; screw to the battens at 2 ft centres.
  4. Cross-brace every leg taller than 1.5 ft, both ways.
  5. Skin the tops with ply; leave hatches A, B, C loose.
  6. Skirting on the open front and left edges with Velcro (removable).

Before you start

  • Measure the real deck height, size and load rating; the leg heights assume a 2 ft deck.
  • Mark the 9 × 16 ft grid on the deck with tape.
  • Number every 4 × 4 ft frame section so it can be put back in the same place.

Check before cutting

Deck height 2 ft and a free 9 × 16 ft are assumed. Re-cut the leg heights if the deck is different.

Done when (tick each)

  • Frame is level across and rigid: two people can stand on any panel with no flex.
  • Service hatches A (control box), B (angel and mister) and C (speaker) open freely and are labelled.
  • Skirting on the open front and left edges hides the deck completely.
  • Every section passes through the church door.

Hands over to

Everything stands on A. Blocks B, C, E, G, K go on after A is placed. H uses the hatches.
