How to build it, in order

  1. Cut the cave inside the hill block (see block D): 4 × 2.5 ft interior, arch 3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high.
  2. Mesh the arch and overhangs, coat with PoP + PVA 3 mm, press grout and pebbles on while wet (same method as D).
  3. Paint the inside near-black so the gold glows; paint the outside sand, then ochre, one shade darker than the rock around.
  4. Hide a warm COB LED above the lintel; keep its wires out of the plaster and run them to hatch A.
  5. Make or repaint the manger (thermocol + plaster) and the clay lamp; lay straw.
  6. Place the figures last: Mary (Mary blue, limestone veil), Joseph (ochre-umber), baby on warm-white straw, ox and donkey beside.
  7. Mark the position of the cross-shadow lamp for the 3:00 cue before closing the back.
  8. Make sure the cave lifts out as its own piece that fits through the church door.
