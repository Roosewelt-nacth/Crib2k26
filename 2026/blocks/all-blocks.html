How to build it, in order

  1. Glue 4 in thermocol into a block (about 7 × 6 × 3 ft); seal every layer with PVA.
  2. Carve strata and cracks with a hot knife; cut the cave 4 × 2.5 ft inside with an arch 3 ft wide × 2.5 ft high.
  3. Mesh the arch and overhangs; coat everything with PoP + PVA, 3 mm; press grout, pebbles and dry grass on while wet.
  4. Dry-brush sand, then ochre, then olive; darker in the cracks. Paint the cave inside near-black so the gold glows; keep the rock around the cave one shade darker.
  5. Hide a warm COB LED above the lintel; keep its wires out of the plaster and run them to hatch A. Leave a 1 ft service gap behind.
  6. Make or repaint the manger (thermocol + plaster) and the clay lamp; lay straw.
  7. Place the figures last: Mary (Mary blue, limestone veil), Joseph (ochre-umber), baby on warm-white straw, ox and donkey beside.
  8. Mark the cross-shadow lamp position for the 3:00 cue, then split the whole unit into numbered pieces that pass through the church door.
