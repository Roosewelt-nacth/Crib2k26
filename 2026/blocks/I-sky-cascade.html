How to build it, in order

  1. Mark hang points with a plumb line; confirm the ceiling type and anchors.
  2. Build the 2 ft star on a foam core with tinsel and a 10 W LED.
  3. Hang 60 bead strands (6 ft) from a 1.8 ft ring.
  4. Wind the wire helix 2.5 ft wide; skin it with polyfill; spray fire retardant.
  5. Attach 30 angels and micro-LED strings; hang from 7 points.
  6. Test at floor level first; hang last; trim after lighting.

Before you start

  • Measure the ceiling height and check its type (concrete, beam or plasterboard); this decides the anchors and the line lengths.
  • Ask the church about working at height and scaffold use.

Check before cutting

Ceiling ≈ 15 ft is an assumption. If higher, strands and lines get longer; if plasterboard, use toggle anchors.

Done when (tick each)

  • Built and lit at floor level first; hung last.
  • Two lines per hang point, never a single line.
  • Spiral points down towards the manger; star is the highest point from V.
  • Cascade lines stay 2 m clear of any sambrani holder.
