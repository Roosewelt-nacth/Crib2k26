How to build it, in order

  1. Cut house blocks (heights 2.0, 2.5, 1.8 ft); cut windows and doors.
  2. Add wood lintels and the outside stair on the tallest house.
  3. Plaster, lime-wash, stain with a thin brown glaze.
  4. Pave the street with cut cork or grooved clay; add a drainage channel.
  5. Fit lamps in the windows; run wires through the base to hatch A.
  6. Place census scene and donkeys after the street is dry.
